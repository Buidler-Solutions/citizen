# Citizen Manifesto

**Citizen** is a civic-first distributed ledger dedicated to public truth, durable record keeping, and democratic accountability. We assert that decentralized infrastructure must be governed by people and institutions, not speculation markets.

## Core Commitments

1. **Public Truth Over Profit** – Records exist to serve communities, regulators, journalists, and historians. We reject price-driven token dynamics in favor of verifiable civic data.
2. **Governance Before Incentives** – Validator power is identity anchored, approved by supermajority vote, and accountable through transparent governance. No anonymous validators, ever.
3. **Deterministic Finality** – Finality occurs in one block with >2/3 validator agreement. Truth is immediate and irreversible.
4. **Sybil Resistance by Design** – Admission is curated through public institutions, legal entities, or bonded commitments. Scale without identity inflation.
5. **Namespaces for Civic Domains** – Land registries, identity anchors, audits, and governance each run inside dedicated namespaces with explicit rules and proofs.
6. **Open, Inspectable Infrastructure** – Code, simulation harnesses, and governance records are open source, reproducible, and auditable.

## Pillars of the Citizen Network

### Governance-Driven Evolution
Citizen evolves through public proposals voted by validators with >2/3 approval. Every change is transparent, time-bounded, and immutable in history.

### Validator Stewardship
Validators are stewards, not miners. They maintain availability, sign honestly, rotate leadership, and uphold civic commitments. Suspension or removal is swift when duty fails.

### Resilient Performance
One-second blocks, global validator sets, and deterministic finality ensure liveness without compromising safety—even under adverse network conditions or partial validator failures.

### Civic Infrastructure Toolbox
Namespaces empower cities, agencies, and communities to run:
- `identity.citizen` for privacy-preserving identifiers
- `registry.land` for property ownership transfers
- `audit.procurement` for public contract trails
- `governance.vote` for protocol amendments
- `supplychain.batch` for traceable goods

Citizen is the backbone for transparent governance infrastructure—extensible without ever subordinating public truth to token volatility.

## Social Contract

- **Transparency:** All governance actions, validator admissions, and emergency powers are on-chain and signed.
- **Inclusivity:** Admission prioritizes diverse public institutions to avoid capture by any single geography or interest group.
- **Accountability:** Civil society can audit every block, vote, and namespace rule; observers run full verification with zero signing power.
- **Sustainability:** Upgrades prioritize safety, auditability, and long-term maintenance over feature churn.

## Call to Action

Citizen invites governments, universities, NGOs, journalists, and technologists to co-steward a shared public ledger. Build open registries, civic audit trails, and governance systems that outlast administrations and market cycles.

Public truth is an infrastructure choice. With Citizen, we choose accountability.
